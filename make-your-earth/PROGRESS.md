# Progress

Milestones re-scoped by the author: 5-player browser co-op, no accounts, servers or voice.

- [x] **M0 Setup:**
  - Vite + TypeScript + Three.js app;
  - Vercel project `make-your-earth` live at https://make-your-earth.vercel.app;
  - Vitest unit tests and a Playwright smoke test.
- [x] **M1 Main menu:**
  - animated shader Earth with clouds, atmosphere and parallax;
  - Play, Friends, Settings and Credits tabs;
  - EN / PT switch;
  - settings with auto-detected presets, separate render and simulation distance, toggles, audio, key rebinding and look options.
- [x] **M2 World:**
  - 8 biomes, rivers, caves, ores, lava;
  - worker meshing;
  - walk, sprint, crouch, swim, climb;
  - axe, chopping and mining with tool tiers;
  - placing blocks;
  - inventory (36 + 9 + armour + off-hand) with drag, shift-click and split;
  - recipe crafting at hand, workbench, furnace, campfire, anvil and lab;
  - map (M) with coordinates and waypoints.
- [x] **M3 Survival:**
  - hunger, thirst, stamina, temperature, sickness and cures;
  - 30 animal species with AI, hunting and drops;
  - downed → grave-chest → new character;
  - day/night, rain, snow and storms;
  - CC0 sounds and ambience.
- [~] **M4 Multiplayer (re-scoped):**
  - **Done:**
    - host/join by room code, max 5 players;
    - friend codes with online status and invites;
    - spawn together;
    - text chat with filter;
    - revive.
  - **Not built:** accounts, shards, voice and the server load test, because the author dropped them.

    - world structures with loot;
    - explosives.
  - **Not yet:** fire spread, land claims.

- [x] **Survival update (caves, monsters, farming, combat):**
  - damage feedback: red flash, screen shake, head tilt, grunt and thud on every hit;
  - three layers of cave tunnels, huge caverns with underground lakes, lava pools, glowing crystals, mushrooms and cobwebs; ravines; tunnels that open to the surface;
  - ores repainted as chunky, shaded minerals with a shape per ore;
  - monsters: zombies, skeleton archers, skeleton warriors, giant and cave spiders, cave beasts and bandits; night and dark-cave spawning blocked by light; undead burn in daylight; health bars and knockback;
  - new animals: duck, wild cat, octopus; tame wolves into dogs (they fight for you) and cats; saddle and ride horses, camels and zebras;
  - bows and arrows, off-hand shields;
  - farming (hoe, farmland, wheat, carrots and potatoes in three stages, bone meal), wild carrots, potatoes, pumpkins and cornflowers;
  - chests with real storage and a furnace with fuel, input and output, shared in co-op;
  - beds (spawn point, skip the night), two-block doors that open and close, windows, stained glass, iron bars, lanterns;
  - about 55 new blocks and 25 new items: coloured wool, concrete, terracotta and glass, nine dyes, three new planks, polished stone, crates, barrels and new foods;
  - graphics: corner shadows (ambient occlusion), swaying leaves and plants, sparkling water, drifting clouds; fixed texture bleed on some blocks.
- [x] **Worlds, crafting and mods update:**
  - five new biomes: swamp (pools, vine trees), taiga (pine forest floor), autumn forest (orange and red trees, pumpkins), red canyon (stepped mesas with striped terracotta) and volcanic fields (ash, basalt, magma, lava pools, charred trees);
  - eight new structures: mine shafts with a room underground, campsites, stone circles, fishing docks, swamp shacks on stilts, volcanic altars, windmills (also in villages) and log cabins;
  - crafting grid (2×2 by hand, 3×3 at stations) with a recipe book that fills it;
  - Block Maker moved to a Designer's Bench;
  - admin panel hidden (F2 ×3, or five taps on the coordinates) and locked with a PIN; item picker with icons;
  - mods: a Mods tab, five official mods, owner-only publishing, try-before-publishing in the admin panel, MODS.md;
  - fixed: painted blocks with see-through pixels made the ground next to them invisible.
