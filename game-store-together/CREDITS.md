## Notes on changes

- Every model was re-compressed for the web with meshopt (gltf-transform). Geometry was **not** simplified.
- Kenney mini-market shelves ship with baked-in products; the game hides those and shows your real stock instead.
- The customers' walk cycle comes from Polyfork's *Family Road-Tripper* and is shared by all Polyfork people (same rig). Sitting, typing, carrying and arcade poses are procedural.
- Player and staff clothes are recoloured at runtime from the Kenney retro character atlas.
- The drinks fridge, PC towers, neon signs, posters, hats, the shop building and all product props (game boxes, plushies, controllers…) are built in code.
